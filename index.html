A little, often.

Read the staff, choose a letter and accidental, then tap Check answer. For chords, choose the root letter and chord type.

Use Choose notes on Home or in App settings to select the exact names you want. Naturals, Sharps, and Flats are separate groups; selecting C♯ does not select D♭. In chord mode, these choices are chord roots.

Name what’s written: a C♯ is answered C♯, even though D♭ sounds the same. Use Staff range · octaves in Choose notes to practise the same name at different heights, including ledger lines. C4 is middle C; C5 sits in the third space of the treble staff. Octave numbers aren’t required in your answer.

A wrong answer shows the played note in red for two seconds, then lets you try the same question again. A correct answer turns green and moves on. Skip reveals the name briefly and moves to another question. Round accuracy counts first tries. Missed items return after a few questions or in a later round.

Short recall sessions and spaced review give you another chance to remember. Progress is kept separately for each clef and written name.

No microphone or account needed. An instrument is optional: enable a MIDI keyboard in App settings, then play and release the displayed note or chord. Touch answers are always available. This is a staff-reading quiz; using a screen reader will describe the prompt but cannot replace the visual reading exercise.
