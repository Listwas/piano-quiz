A QUICK GUIDE

How Piano Quiz works

Today suggests one exercise. Practice lets you choose notes, chords, rhythm, sight-reading or the metronome. Progress keeps the results and lets you replay difficult bars.

When tapping answers, choose the written name without an octave. With MIDI, play the exact pitch and octave. Hold a chord together and release it to check.

Practice retries a failed sight-reading bar after a count-in. Flow keeps the beat moving and shows the next bar early.

Progress stays in this browser. Settings lets you export it before switching devices.
